A. Srinivas & P. Krishna Reddy (JDSA 2021)

⏱️ Focus: 15:00
1. From Frequent Graphs to Maximum Coverage

Finding Minimal Sets of Molecular Subgraphs for Maximum Target Reach

Traditional Frequent Subgraph Mining (gSpan, FSG) searches for small subgraphs that occur in many molecular graphs. But in drug discovery or vulnerability detection, researchers do not just want one recurring subgraph—they want a combination of diverse subgraphs that collectively COVER the target database with minimal overlap!

🧪 The Molecular Graph Challenge: Graph isomorphism (subgraph matching) is NP-complete. Finding combinations of subgraphs that optimize set union coverage while managing canonical DFS codes is a daunting computational challenge.

A. Srinivas and P. Krishna Reddy (JDSA 2021) at IIIT Hyderabad developed the theory of Subgraph Coverage Patterns, extending coverage semantics to graph databases.
2. Mathematical Formalism

Graph Coverage Sets & Non-Overlapping Subgraph Unions

Subgraph Coverage Formulation:
Let D = {G_1, G_2, ..., G_N} be a database of graphs.
For a candidate subgraph g: TID(g) = { G_i ∈ D | g ⊆ G_i }

Coverage Set of Subgraph Collection S = {g_1, g_2, ..., g_k}:
CSet(S) = ∪_{g ∈ S} TID(g)

Coverage Support (CS):
CS(S) = |CSet(S)| / |D| (Fraction of graphs containing at least one subgraph in S)

Overlap Ratio (OR):
OR(S) = (∑ |TID(g_i)| - |CSet(S)|) / |CSet(S)|

Test Your Understanding

1. How does Subgraph Coverage mining differ from traditional Frequent Subgraph mining?

Explanation: Correct! The objective is broad union reach with low redundancy.

2. What is a Canonical DFS Code in graph mining algorithms like gSpan?

Explanation: Correct! Canonical DFS codes uniquely identify isomorphic graphs without costly pairwise isomorphism tests.

3. What does an Overlap Ratio (OR) of 0.0 mean for a set of subgraphs?

Explanation: Correct! Zero overlap indicates maximum diversity and minimal redundancy.

4. Why can Apriori downward closure NOT be applied directly to graph coverage support?

Explanation: Correct! Union coverage grows as more items are added, breaking the anti-monotonic property of intersection support.

5. How does Srinivas & Reddy's algorithm enable pruning without downward closure?

Explanation: Correct! Sorted item ordering enables upper bounding on overlap and coverage.
