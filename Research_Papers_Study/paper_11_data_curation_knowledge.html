1. From Unstructured Text to Structured Truth

Building Enterprise Knowledge Graphs at Web Scale

Web searches used to return 10 blue links matching keywords. Today, search engines provide direct structured knowledge cards: "Albert Einstein was born in Ulm, Germany on March 14, 1879, won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921, and died in Princeton."

🌐 The Knowledge Graph Paradigm: Knowledge Graphs represent human knowledge as formal semantic triples:
$$\langle\text{Subject}, \text{Predicate}, \text{Object} \r\rangle$$ Examples: $\langle\text{Einstein}, \text{bornIn}, \text{Ulm} \rangle$, $\langle\text{Apple Inc.}, \text{foundedBy}, \text{Steve Jobs} \rangle$.

Gerhard Weikum, Luna Dong, Simon Razniewski, and Fabian Suchanek (ACM SIGMOD Record 2021) provide the definitive survey on the 4-phase lifecycle of machine knowledge creation and continuous curation.
2. Theory & The 4-Stage Lifecycle

Extraction, Canonicalization, Validation, & Temporal Scoping

1. Information Extraction (IE)

Extracting candidate entities and relations from raw text using Named Entity Recognition (NER) and Open Information Extraction (OpenIE).

2. Entity Canonicalization & Disambiguation

Resolving ambiguous surface strings. Does "Paris" mean the capital of France (Q90), Paris Hilton (Q47899), or Paris, Texas? Mapping text mentions to unique Wikidata IDs.

3. Ontological Validation & Constraint Checking

Enforcing logic constraints: A person can have only one birthdate (functional property); birth date must precede death date; spouses must be humans.

4. Temporal Knowledge Scoping

Facts change over time. Triples must be scoped with temporal validity intervals: $\langle\text{Barack Obama}, \text{presidentOf}, \text{USA} angle [2009, 2017]$.

4. Active Retention Quiz

Test Your Understanding

1. What is the fundamental structure used to represent facts in a Knowledge Graph?

Explanation: Correct! The subject-predicate-object triple forms the basic atom of knowledge representation.

2. What is Entity Canonicalization?

Explanation: Correct! Canonicalization resolves synonyms and variants to a canonical entity node.

3. How are temporal changes handled in modern Knowledge Graphs?

Explanation: Correct! Facts like "CEO of" or "President of" require validity windows.

4. What role do SHACL / OWL ontological constraints play in knowledge curation?

Explanation: Correct! Logic constraints prevent corrupt, hallucinated, or contradictory facts from entering the KG.

5. Which famous public Knowledge Graph was pioneered by Gerhard Weikum and Fabian Suchanek in 2007?

Explanation: Correct! YAGO combined Wikipedia facts with WordNet taxonomic rigor, paving the way for the Google Knowledge Graph.
