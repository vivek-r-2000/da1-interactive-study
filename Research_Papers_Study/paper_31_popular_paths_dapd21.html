Discovering Popular Paths Using Transactional Modeling

Ravanth & P. Krishna Reddy (DAPD 2021)

⏱️ Focus: 15:00
1. Urban Traffic Engineering

Discovering Popular Travel Corridors from Millions of GPS Pings

City planners and navigation apps receive millions of GPS pings from taxis and rideshares. Planners want to know: "What are the most popular contiguous road corridors traveled between city centers and suburbs?"

🗺️ The Algorithmic Hurdle: Vehicles start and end at arbitrary locations. Raw GPS points contain jitter and unequal ping rates. A naive enumeration of all possible road path sub-sequences causes exponential combinatorial explosion!

Ravanth and P. Krishna Reddy (DAPD 2021) at IIIT Hyderabad solved this by mapping GPS traces to contiguous road network segments and discovering popular paths via Transactional Modeling without combinatorial search.
2. Mathematical Formalism

Contiguous Segment Modeling & Prefix Tree Mining

Road Network Representation:
Graph G = (V, E), where E is the set of directed road segments.

Trip Transaction Representation:
Each vehicle journey is modeled as an ordered contiguous sequence: Trip_k = ⟨e_1, e_2, ..., e_m⟩ where head(e_i) = tail(e_{i+1}).

Popular Path Criterion:
A contiguous sub-path P = ⟨e_a, ..., e_b⟩ is Popular if its Support ≥ min_path_sup across trips, with no sub-corridor redundancy.
