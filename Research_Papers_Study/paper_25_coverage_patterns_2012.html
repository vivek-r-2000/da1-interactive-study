Mining Coverage Patterns for Banner Advertisement Placement

P. Gowtham Srinivas, P. Krishna Reddy et al. (2012)

⏱️ Focus: 15:00
1. The Online Advertising Paradox

Why Supermarket Frequent Mining Fails in Web Advertising

Supermarkets want to know which products customers buy together. But online advertisers want the exact opposite: an advertiser buying 3 banner ad slots wants their brand seen by as many DIFFERENT, UNIQUE users as possible!

🥨 The Beginner's Analogy: If an ad is shown on 'Football News' AND 'Cricket News', the same sports fan sees the ad 10 times. You wasted your budget showing ads to the same person. Placing one ad on 'Sports' and another on 'Cooking Recipes' covers 90% of all internet visitors with almost zero audience overlap.

3. Core Theory & CMine Algorithm

The Sorted Closure Property & CMine

1. Why Downward Closure Fails

Coverage Support is based on set UNION ($\cup$). As you add pages to set $X$, $|CSet(X)|$ monotonically increases! An infrequent single page can combine with others to exceed minCS, so standard Apriori pruning fails completely.

2. The Sorted Closure Breakthrough

Srinivas and Krishna Reddy proved: Sort pages in decreasing order of frequency. The Non-Overlap Pattern (NOP) property ($OR \le \text{maxOR}$) RESTORES downward closure! If a sorted pattern has overlap $> \text{maxOR}$, ALL supersets also violate maxOR and are pruned!

4. Midsem Practice Quiz

Exam Retention Check

Q1. In the CMine coverage pattern algorithm, why MUST web pages be sorted in decreasing order of frequency?

Correct! The sorted closure property proves that sorting by decreasing frequency guarantees that if any subset violates maxOR, all supersets also violate it, enabling level-wise candidate pruning.

Test Your Understanding

1. Why does traditional frequent itemset mining fail for banner advertisement placement?

Explanation: Correct! Placing ads on co-occurring pages shows the same ad repeatedly to the same user, wasting budget.

2. What does an Overlap Ratio (OR) of 0.0 indicate between two pages?

Explanation: Correct! Zero overlap indicates maximum advertising diversification.

3. What is a Non-Overlap Pattern (NOP)?

Explanation: Correct! NOPs satisfy the maximum overlap constraint.

4. Why does CMine sort items in decreasing order of frequency?

Explanation: Correct! The sorted closure property enables level-wise pruning despite the non-anti-monotonic nature of set unions.

5. What is the definition of Coverage Support (CS)?

Explanation: Correct! Coverage support measures the cardinality of the UNION of visitor sets.
