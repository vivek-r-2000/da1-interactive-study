1. Continuous Trajectories to Discrete Insights

Mining Periodic Trajectories in Spatial Surveillance Networks

Maritime vessels, patrol drones, and migrating wildlife move across continuous space and time. Surveillance stations (radar, optical towers) need to answer visibility queries: "Which vessels exhibit periodic, repeating visibility across our network of radar towers?"

🛰️ The Modeling Challenge: Continuous geometric trajectories cannot be fed into standard market-basket algorithms.

P. Radha Krishna, P. Krishna Reddy et al. (2019) introduced Portion-Based Transactional Modeling to discretize continuous spatio-temporal visibility intervals into transactional matrices, enabling high-speed periodic pattern discovery.
2. Mathematical Formalism

Portion Time Windows & Periodic Visibility

Portion-Based Representation:
Time is divided into regular cycles of length L (e.g. 24 hours), subdivided into discrete portions p ∈ {1, 2, ..., M}.

Portion Transaction T_p:
T_p = { Object_ID | Object is visible in sensor field during portion p }

Periodic Support Metric:
A pattern of moving objects is periodic with period P if its visibility recurs across regular cycle portions with confidence ≥ min_periodic_conf.
