## 📖 Overview

A visual, bite-sized, ADHD-friendly interactive learning platform for **Data Analytics I (DA-1)**. 
Built completely from first principles with zero assumed prerequisites, featuring interactive algorithmic animations, dynamic parameters, step-by-step simulations, formula translations, and retention quizzes.

---

## 📱 Mobile-First Architecture

- **Off-Canvas Slide-Out Navigation (`☰ Menu`):** Quick jump to any section with your thumb.
- **Thumb-Friendly Touch Navigation:** Full-width bottom navigation bars (`min-height: 42px`).
- **Zero Horizontal Overflow:** All canvas graphics, formula tables, and SVG diagrams adapt seamlessly to phone viewports (iPhone, Android).
- **Embedded Pomodoro Sprints:** 15-minute focus timers built into every lesson.

## 📚 Curriculum Structure

### 📘 8 Core Lecture Course Modules
1. **[Module 01: Introduction to Data Analytics](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/01_intro.html)** — DIKW Pyramid, KDD 8-step pipeline, 6 data mining superpowers, email classifier.
2. **[Module 02: Data Concepts & Statistics](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/02_data_concepts_stats.html)** — Attribute taxonomies, 5 Vs of Big Data, Boxplots, and live Pearson Correlation explorer.
3. **[Module 03: Data Preprocessing & Similarity](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/03_preprocessing.html)** — Euclidean vs Manhattan distances, Jaccard sets, Missing value imputation, and live PCA vector projection.
4. **[Module 04: Data Warehousing & OLAP](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/04_warehousing_olap.html)** — Star vs Snowflake schema, 3D interactive cube operations (Roll-up, Drill-down, Slice, Dice), Attribute-Oriented Induction (AOI).
5. **[Module 05: Data Cube Algorithms](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/05_data_cube_algorithms.html)** — Bottom-Up Computation (BUC) lattice pruning, MultiWay array chunking, and Star-Cubing.
6. **[Module 06: Association Rule Mining](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/06_association_rules.html)** — Support & Confidence, Apriori candidate join-prune animator, FP-Tree construction, Lift & Kulczynski metrics.
7. **[Module 07: Advanced Pattern Mining](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/07_advanced_patterns.html)** — Coverage Patterns (CMine), Multi-level taxonomies, High-Utility mining with TWU, and constraint classifications.
8. **[Module 08: Introduction to Classification](https://vivek-r-2000.github.io/da1-interactive-study/Study_Material/08_classification.html)** — Decision Trees (Information Gain/Entropy/Gini), Naive Bayes spam filter, k-NN playground, interactive Confusion Matrix, ROC curves, and Ensembles.

### 🔬 35 Seminal Research Paper Tutorials
Full theoretical breakdowns with mathematical notation and interactive sandboxes covering:
- **Foundations & KDD Theory:** Ackoff (DIKW), Özsu (Data Science), Mannila (Inductive Databases), Cao, Markus & Saunders.
- **Streaming, Multimodal & Truth Discovery:** Kumar et al. (COVID Multimodal Fusion), Domingos & Hulten (VFDT / Hoeffding), Oliveira (Visual Mining), Yin & Han (TruthFinder).
- **Data Curation & Cleaning:** Knowledge Graphs, Similarity Joins, Human-in-the-Loop, Potter's Wheel, Sequential Dependencies.
- **Warehousing, Cube & Aggregates:** Han & Cai (AOI), Jim Gray (Data Cube Operator), VLDB 96 Multidimensional Aggregates, MultiWay Array (SIGMOD 97), BUC (SIGMOD 99).
- **Frequent Itemsets & Association:** AIS, Apriori (VLDB 94), FP-Tree (SIGMOD 00), ECLAT (TKDE 00).
- **Advanced Patterns:** Coverage Patterns, Multi-Level Mining, High-Utility (TWU), Quantitative Rules, Diverse Patterns, Periodic Patterns, Popular Paths, Subgraph Coverage, High Court Legal OLAP.
- **Classical Surveys:** Top 10 Algorithms in Data Mining (Wu & Kumar), 50 Years of Decision Trees (Wei-Yin Loh).

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Rendering Engine:** Vanilla HTML5, CSS3, ES6 JavaScript (zero heavy node dependencies at runtime).
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS + custom dark-mode glassmorphism variables.
- **Math:** KaTeX with client-side auto-render.
- **Graphics:** HTML5 Canvas API + SVG interactive vectors.
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages with automated continuous deployment.
