A Two-Phase Algorithm for Fast Discovery of High Utility Itemsets

Ying Liu, Wei-keng Liao, Alok Choudhary (PAKDD 2005)

⏱️ Focus: 15:00
1. The Business Paradox

When 10 Luxury Items Make More Money Than 5,000 Cheap Ones

Traditional frequent itemset mining assumes all items are equally valuable and bought in binary quantities ($0$ or $1$). In real business, selling 10 luxury laptops generates $10,000 profit, while selling 1,000 boxes of toothpicks generates only $100 profit. Apriori finds the toothpicks and misses the laptops!

🥨 The Beginner's Analogy: A restaurant manager evaluates dish success not by how many napkins were used, but by total dollar revenue. High-Utility Itemset Mining discovers patterns based on actual dollar profit (Internal Quantity $\times$ External Unit Profit).

3. Core Theoretical Proof

Why TWU Restores Downward Closure

1. The Anti-Monotone Failure of Utility

Utility $u(X)$ is NOT anti-monotonic. Adding caviar to a bread basket INCREASES total profit. If you prune {Bread} because its utility is low, you will illegally miss {Bread, Caviar} which has astronomical utility!

2. The TWDC Property (Transaction-Weighted Downward Closure)

Liu et al. defined $TWU(X)$: the sum of ALL transaction profits for transactions containing $X$. They proved that $TWU$ is an over-estimation upper bound ($u(X) \le TWU(X)$) that DOES satisfy downward closure! If $TWU(X) < \text{min\_util}$, no superset of $X$ can possibly be high utility!

3. The Two-Phase Pipeline

• Phase 1: Uses $TWU$ with Apriori level-wise candidate generation to create an over-estimated candidate pool of High TWU Itemsets (HTWUIs).
• Phase 2: Performs ONE final database scan to calculate exact utilities $u(X)$ and remove false positives!

4. Midsem Practice Quiz

Exam Retention Check

Q1. Why does Phase 1 of the Two-Phase algorithm use TWU instead of raw itemset utility u(X)?

Correct! Raw utility violates downward closure. TWU acts as an upper bound that restores downward closure, allowing level-wise candidate pruning without generating false negatives.
5. Active Retention Quiz

Test Your Understanding

1. Why can Apriori pruning NOT be applied directly to exact itemset utility?

Explanation: Correct! A subset can have low utility while its superset has high utility (e.g. adding expensive caviar increases the bundle utility).

2. What is Transaction-Weighted Utilization (TWU)?

Explanation: Correct! TWU over-estimates exact utility and satisfies the Transaction-Weighted Downward Closure (TWDC) property.

3. What does Phase 1 of the Two-Phase algorithm output?

Explanation: Correct! Phase 1 over-estimates to generate candidates safely without missing any true high-utility pattern.

4. What does Phase 2 of the Two-Phase algorithm do?

Explanation: Correct! Phase 2 eliminates the over-estimated candidates whose exact profit doesn't meet min_util.

5. What is External Utility vs Internal Utility?

Explanation: Correct! Utility of item i in transaction t is internal utility q(i, t) multiplied by external utility p(i).
