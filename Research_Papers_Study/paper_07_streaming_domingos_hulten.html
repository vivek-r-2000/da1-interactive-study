Mining High-Speed Data Streams

Pedro Domingos & Geoff Hulten (ACM SIGKDD 2000)

⏱️ Focus: 15:00
1. Infinite Streams & Bounded Memory

Building Decision Trees Over Infinite Streams Without Storing Data

Classical algorithms like C4.5 and CART assume training datasets fit in RAM or can be scanned multiple times from disk. But internet routers, IoT sensors, and financial tickers generate millions of events per second that never stop.

⚡ The Core Challenge: How many examples do you need to observe before you can be mathematically certain which attribute is the optimal split?

Pedro Domingos and Geoff Hulten (ACM SIGKDD 2000) at the University of Washington proved how to use the Hoeffding Bound to construct VFDT (Very Fast Decision Tree): a streaming learner that processes each example in $O(1)$ time and guarantees tree structure asymptotically identical to batch algorithms!
2. Mathematical Formalism

The Hoeffding Bound & Node Splitting Criteria

The Hoeffding bound provides a distribution-free mathematical guarantee for estimating true means from random samples.

The Hoeffding Bound Equation:
Let r be a random variable with range R (e.g. Information Gain in [0, log2(C)]).
After observing n independent examples with sample mean r_bar:

ε = √ [ (R² × ln(1 / δ)) / (2n) ]

With probability (1 - δ), the true mean of r is at least: r_bar - ε.

VFDT Splitting Rule:
Let G(X_1) be the best attribute and G(X_2) be the second-best attribute observed at a leaf.
If: ΔG = G(X_1) - G(X_2) > ε
Then: We are (1 - δ) confident that X_1 is the true best attribute! Split the leaf immediately!
⚖️ Tie Breaking (τ): If two features are virtually identical in quality ($\Delta G < \epsilon$ for huge $n$), waiting for $\epsilon \to 0$ wastes CPU. If $\epsilon < \tau$, VFDT splits immediately on either feature to keep tree expansion moving.

4. Active Retention Quiz

Test Your Understanding

1. Why does the Hoeffding bound not require knowing the probability distribution of the data?

Explanation: Correct! The bound holds regardless of underlying distribution, making it ideal for streaming where distributions are unknown.

2. In VFDT, what happens to raw stream tuples after updating attribute count statistics at a leaf?

Explanation: Correct! Storing raw data would violate the bounded-memory guarantee of infinite streaming.

3. What does the parameter δ represent in the Hoeffding bound?

Explanation: Correct! Typical values are 10^-5 to 10^-7, guaranteeing 99.999% split accuracy.

4. What is the purpose of the tie-breaking threshold τ?

Explanation: Correct! Tie-breaking prevents deadlock when two features produce identical information gain.

5. What extension was developed later by Hulten, Spencer, and Domingos to handle concept drift?

Explanation: Correct! CVFDT actively prunes and replaces subtrees when underlying data distributions drift.
