1. Context & Purpose

Data Science as a Discipline: The Whole-of-Life Analytics Roadmap

Assigned in Professor Krishna Reddy's syllabus for Quiz 1, Longbing Cao's survey in ACM Computing Surveys 2017 is the definitive, authoritative blueprint of Data Science. It bridges raw computer science, statistics, social science, and business decision-making.

🥨 The Beginner's Analogy: Think of running a commercial airline:
  • Descriptive (Past): "Flight 402 was delayed 4 hours yesterday." (Reporting facts).
  • Diagnostic (Past Why): "The delay happened because of unpredicted hydraulic pump seal wear." (Root-cause analysis).
  • Predictive (Future): "Aircraft #12 has an 87% chance of pump failure in the next 15 flight hours." (Forecasting).
  • Prescriptive (Action): "Automatically re-route Aircraft #12 to the Dallas maintenance depot tonight and schedule parts replacement before failure occurs." (Optimal decision).

3. Core Exam Concepts

Quiz 1 Key Takeaways: Cao's Life-Cycle Paradigm

1. Data DNA and Non-IID Data

Traditional statistics assumes data points are IID (Independent and Identically Distributed). Cao demonstrates that modern Big Data is non-IID: entities have complex couplings, behavioral hierarchies, and temporal relationships that require relational graph mining.

2. Actionable Knowledge vs. Academic Accuracy

A classifier with 99% accuracy on paper is useless if its predictions cannot be acted upon by human operators. Cao introduces the concept of Actionable Knowledge Discovery (AKD): patterns must satisfy technical interestingness (support, confidence, lift) AND business utility (profit, policy compliance).

4. Self-Assessment Quiz

Retention Check

Q1. A hospital analytics system predicts which patients will be readmitted, AND calculates the optimal home-nursing schedule to prevent readmission. What category of analytics is the second function?

Correct! Prescriptive analytics goes beyond forecasting the future; it optimizes and recommends the best specific action/policy to take.
