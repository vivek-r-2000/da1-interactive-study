1. Epistemology of Data Science

Is Data Science a Real Discipline or Just Applied Statistics?

For years, skeptics labeled Data Science as mere marketing rebranding for applied statistics or computer programming.

🎓 The Definitive Academic Answer: M. Tamer Özsu (Communications of the ACM 2023) at the University of Waterloo published Data Science: A Systematic Treatment to establish Data Science as a rigorous, distinct academic discipline founded upon three interlocked pillars:

1. Computational Thinking: Scalable distributed systems, databases, index structures, complexity.
2. Inferential & Statistical Thinking: Uncertainty estimation, hypothesis testing, causal inference.
3. Domain Epistemology: Translating real-world societal, biological, or business challenges into computational questions.
2. Pipeline Architecture & Governance

The Complete Scientific Lifecycle

Özsu emphasizes that model training is only a tiny fraction of Data Science. The real intellectual work lies in data curation, provenance tracking, reproducibility, and ethical governance.

3. Analytical Modeling Statistics & Machine Learning Overfitting, data leakage, spurious correlation
