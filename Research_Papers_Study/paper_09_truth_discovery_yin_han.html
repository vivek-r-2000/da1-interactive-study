1. Conflicting Web Data

Discovering Truth in a Web of Rumors, Scrapers, and Conflicts

On the World Wide Web, information is frequently contradictory. Multiple online bookstores list completely different authors for the same book; medical forums give contradictory dosage advice.

⚠️ Why Simple Majority Voting Fails: One inaccurate blog post is often scraped by 50 cheap automated scraper websites. If an algorithm uses simple democratic majority voting, the false claim wins 50 to 1!

Xiaoxin Yin, Jiawei Han, and Philip S. Yu (IEEE TKDE 2007) introduced TruthFinder, the foundational algorithm for Truth Discovery based on bipartite mutual reinforcement.
2. Mathematical Formalism

Bipartite Graphs & Log-Likelihood Damping

Websites $W$ and candidate facts $F$ form a bipartite graph. The fundamental axiom:
"A website is trustworthy if it asserts true facts; a fact is believable if asserted by trustworthy websites."

TruthFinder Iterative Equations:
1. Fact Confidence from Website Trustworthiness:
σ(f) = 1 - ∏_{w ∈ W(f)} (1 - t(w))

2. Website Trustworthiness from Fact Confidence:
t(w) = ∑_{f ∈ F(w)} c(f) / |F(w)|

3. Conflict Damping Factor (ρ):
If fact f1 conflicts with f2 (e.g. Author is John vs Author is Bob):
Score(f1) = Confidence(f1) - ρ × Score(f2)
Negative evidence from authoritative sources actively dampens conflicting false facts!

4. Active Retention Quiz

Test Your Understanding

1. Why does naive majority voting fail in web data integration?

Explanation: Correct! Syndication and copying create artificial majorities for incorrect facts.

2. What graph topology represents the relationship between sources and claims in TruthFinder?

Explanation: Correct! The dual sets of sources and facts form a natural bipartite network.

3. How does conflict damping (ρ) operate in TruthFinder?

Explanation: Correct! Evidence in favor of one fact acts as negative evidence against its mutually exclusive rivals.

4. What algorithmic paradigm does TruthFinder share with Google PageRank and Kleinberg's HITS?

Explanation: Correct! Like Hubs and Authorities in HITS, trustworthiness and confidence reinforce each other iteratively.

5. Can TruthFinder detect copied sources?

Explanation: Correct! Source dependency detection discounts copied answers.
