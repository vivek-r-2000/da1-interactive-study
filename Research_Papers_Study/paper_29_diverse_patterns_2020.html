Mining Diverse Frequent Patterns

B. Swamy, P. Krishna Reddy, B. Kavitha (PAKDD 2020)

⏱️ Focus: 15:00
1. Beyond Monoculture Patterns

Discovering Cross-Category Synergy Across Unbalanced Taxonomies

Standard frequent itemset mining algorithms (Apriori, FP-Growth) discover thousands of redundant patterns that cluster tightly inside the same narrow product niche: {iPhone 12, Lightning Cable, AirPods}.

🛒 The Real Retail Goal: Retailers and recommender systems do not want to recommend 5 accessories for a phone you already bought—they want cross-category diversity that induces customers to explore clothing, groceries, and home appliances.

B. Swamy, P. Krishna Reddy, and B. Kavitha (PAKDD 2020) at IIIT Hyderabad formulated Diverse Frequent Patterns, introducing mathematical projections on unbalanced taxonomies and the Diversity Rank ($drank$) metric.
2. Mathematical Formalism

Subtree Projections & The drank Metric

Given a product taxonomy tree $C$ and an itemset $Y$:

Minimal Projection π(Y / C):
The set of lowest common ancestor category nodes in hierarchy C that span the items in Y.

Maximal Projection m(Y / C):
The set of distinct top-level branch partitions covered by Y.

Diversity Rank Equation:
drank(Y) = |π(Y / C)| / |Y| × Dispersion_Factor(Y, C)
• A pattern where all items belong to a single narrow leaf has drank ≈ 0.0 (Zero Diversity).
• A pattern whose items span independent top-level subtrees achieves maximum drank ≈ 1.0!

Test Your Understanding

1. Why are standard frequent patterns often unhelpful for cross-selling recommendations?

Explanation: Correct! Within-category clustering leads to redundant, low-value recommendations.

2. What does an itemset's Minimal Projection π(Y / C) compute?

Explanation: Correct! It maps specific items up to their tightest common taxonomy parents.

3. What does a Diversity Rank (drank) near 1.0 indicate?

Explanation: Correct! High drank patterns represent cross-category shopping synergies.

4. Why does an UNBALANCED hierarchy present special mathematical challenges for diversity mining?

Explanation: Correct! Depth asymmetry requires normalized projection metrics.

Explanation: Correct! Bounding maximum potential diversity enables early tree pruning.
