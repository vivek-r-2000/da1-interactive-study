1. Applied Judicial Informatics

Combating 45 Million Pending Cases with Multidimensional OLAP

India's judicial system faces an acute crisis: over 45 million cases remain pending across High Courts and District Courts, with citizens waiting decades for case resolution. Chief Justices and law ministries lack systematic multidimensional analytics to identify where and why cases stall.

⚖️ The Multidimensional Solution: Rather than simple spreadsheets of aggregate numbers, judicial administrators need the ability to Slice, Dice, Roll-up, and Drill-down across court hierarchies, case types, procedural stages, and judge benches.

Sriharshitha Bondugula, P. Krishna Reddy, and Justice P. Naveen Rao (Artificial Intelligence and Law 2025) designed a complete Data Cube for Judicial Analytics evaluated on over 100,000 real High Court judgments.
2. Schema Architecture & Formal OLAP Measures

The Judicial Star Schema

Dimension Tables:
  • • Court: [State ➔ High Court ➔ Bench / District]
  • • Time: [Decade ➔ Year ➔ Quarter ➔ Month]
  • • CaseType: [Broad Category ➔ Specific Act / Section]
  • • ProceduralStage: [Filing ➔ Notices ➔ Evidence ➔ Arguments]
Fact Table Measures:
  • • TotalCasesDisposed (Distributive: SUM)
  • • TotalCasesPending (Distributive: SUM)
  • • AverageDelayDays (Algebraic: SUM / COUNT)
  • • AdjournmentFrequency (Algebraic: Adjournments / Case)
